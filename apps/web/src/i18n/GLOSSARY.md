# KPBooks Spanish glossary

Canonical EN → ES terminology. Every translated string MUST use these terms —
consistency across the app matters more than any individual preference.
Register: neutral Latin-American business Spanish (the client base is
US-based, largely Mexican/Central-American construction trades). Use "usted"
implicitly (imperative infinitives or third person), never "tú".

## Easily confused
- **Void ≠ reversal.** *Anular* / *anulado* / *anulación* belong to Void alone —
  cancelling a document. A reversal is the offsetting journal entry, *reversión*
  (`asiento de reversión`). Voiding an invoice writes a reversal, but plain
  reversing entries exist with no void behind them, so never render "reversal"
  as *anulación*.

## Do NOT translate
- Form names: 1099-NEC, 1099-MISC, W-9, W-4, I-9, 941, 940
- "IIF", "QuickBooks", "CSV", "PDF", "KPBooks"
- Account codes, company names, user-entered data
- "sub" jargon may render as "subcontratista"
